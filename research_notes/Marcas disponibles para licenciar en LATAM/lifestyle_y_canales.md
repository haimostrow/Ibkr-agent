# Lifestyle, streetwear, outdoor and fashion brands you can license or distribute in LATAM, and the channels that offer them (as of 26 Nov 2026)

Scope note: this covers non-sportswear apparel, footwear and accessory brands only. The BasicNet brands and heritage sportswear brands (Fila, Umbro, Diadora, Lotto, Le Coq Sportif, Ellesse, Champion, Reebok) are out of scope. They appear below only where a deal shows how a brand manager or agent works in the region. Dates are publication dates where known. Items dated before 2025 are flagged **[pre-2025, possibly outdated]**. Several trade-press pages (License Global, The Licensing Letter, Modaes) returned 403 or 500 when fetched. For those, the facts come from search-result summaries and are marked "(search summary)".

## Q1. Which specific brands announced in 2025–2026 that they are looking for LATAM licensees or distributors?

### Takeaway
I found only a few public, dated calls for LATAM partners in 2025–2026 in this segment. The clearest are **Bench** (UK/Canadian skate-streetwear brand; U.S. + LATAM, all apparel and accessory categories, via The Brand Liaison, April 2025) and **Nicole Miller** (U.S. fashion; first LATAM agent, Tycoon Enterprises, July 2025; launch in 2H 2026, Mexico first). Next is **Dockers**: Authentic Brands Group said it would grow the brand through regional operators and gained Mexico and Colombia rights in April 2026. Most other 2025–2026 news is about deals already closed (Volcom Uruguay, Ciele, U.S. Polo Assn. Brazil). Those deals show which distributors are active, not which territories are open.

### Inferences
- Dated, public "LATAM partner wanted" calls in non-sportswear apparel are rare. Most brands sign quietly through an agent or a brand manager's regional team. A would-be licensee should talk to agents (Tycoon, The Brand Liaison) rather than wait for announcements.
- **Bench** and **Nicole Miller** look like the most actionable 2025–2026 leads. Both were announced as "seeking partners across categories" programs for LATAM, and neither announcement named LATAM licensees afterward. It is unknown whether any were signed later.
- **Dockers in Mexico and Colombia:** Authentic took these rights in April 2026, and the February 2026 IAC deal leaves them out. A Mexico/Colombia operator or licensee may have been named after April 2026 or may still be open. This is not confirmed.
- **Mammut**'s 2026 expansion skipped LATAM, so the region may be open. This is speculative; I found no statement.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025–2026 public LATAM partner searches for named streetwear or DTC athleisure brands such as Vuori, Alo, Gymshark, Represent, Corteiz or Aimé Leon Dore. Their LATAM status is unknown.
- I could not confirm whether Bench or Nicole Miller signed LATAM licensees by November 2026.
- I could not confirm who holds Dockers rights or operations in Mexico and Colombia after April 2026.
- I did not verify whether Smash's licensing plan is still active.

## Q2. Which brand-management companies have LATAM offices or regional agents, and which brands in their portfolios lack a LATAM licensee?

### Takeaway
**Authentic Brands Group** has the strongest LATAM setup: a Mexico City (Polanco) regional hub, a Brazil head, 25 brands and 45 licensees in Mexico, and frequent 2025–2026 deals. **Iconix** runs a LATAM business unit and promoted availability in Peru. **WHP Global**, **Bluestar Alliance** and **Marquee Brands** have no LATAM-specific apparel licensees in the public sources I found. That makes their fashion and streetwear brands (Off-White, Palm Angels, Hurley, Vera Wang, Rag & Bone, Anne Klein, Bonobos, Joe's Jeans, BCBG, Ben Sherman, etc.) worth asking about. Their "no licensee" status is **unknown**, not confirmed.

Their LATAM programs appear thin or undisclosed, so they are the most likely sources of "brands without a LATAM licensee". That is inferred from missing evidence, not confirmed.
- Global Brands Group "successors" did not come up in any LATAM context. I found no evidence that they are a current LATAM channel.

### Gaps

## Q3. Which LATAM licensing agencies represent brands for apparel and footwear, and how does a licensee approach them?

### Takeaway
**Tycoon Enterprises** is the main pan-regional agency signing fashion brands; Nicole Miller (2025) is the notable apparel case. Country specialists include **Versalicensing** (Mexico, apparel), **Timeless Brands** (Monterrey, mostly character and entertainment), **La Panadería Licensing** (Mexico), and in Brazil **Redibra, Lotus Global Marketing, Ziggle Licensing, Destra** and **Vigna Corporate**. In the Southern Cone there are **IMC Licensing** (Argentina), **DOCE** (Chile) and **Spectrum** (Buenos Aires). Many agency sources are older than 2025. Licensees usually reach these agencies at LicensingCon LATAM or Brand Licensing Expo, or by contacting their business-development teams directly. No agency publishes a formal "become a licensee" process in the sources I found.
