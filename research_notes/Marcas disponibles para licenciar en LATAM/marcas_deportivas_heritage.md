# Heritage / retro / mid-market sportswear brands: LATAM licensing and distribution status (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: research done 26 Nov 2026. Items dated before 2025 are flagged **[pre-2025, possibly outdated]**. "Unknown" means no reliable source was found in this research. Some trade-press pages (sgieurope, modaes, fashionnetwork) could not be fetched in full (403/405), so a few facts rest on search-result excerpts; these are marked **[excerpt only]**.

## Q1. For each brand, who holds the LATAM license/distribution today, and is any territory open?

### Takeaway
LATAM is mostly carved up country-by-country. The big Southern Cone football/heritage rights are locked long-term (Umbro and Fila with Grupo Dass in Brazil/Argentina/Paraguay; Umbro through 2035). The most fragmented, and so most likely open, rights sit with brand-management owners: Iconix (Umbro outside the Southern Cone, Starter, Pony), WHP Global (Lotto outside Brazil), Authentic (Reebok/Champion/Prince category licences) and the post-insolvency Le Coq Sportif. Joma, Asics, Puma and (in Mexico) Under Armour and Reebok run their own operations, so they are not realistically available.
